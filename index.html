What's chicory, and why 80:20?

Roasted chicory root gives filter kaapi its body and that thick, frothy davara pour. 80:20 is the classic Bengaluru darshini taste; 70:30 is stronger and darker; Pure Filter has no chicory.

How should I store it?

Squeeze out the air, zip it shut and keep it away from heat and sunlight. Best within 2–3 months of opening.
